Welcome to the **Cookiebot plugin** for Vue and Nuxt applications. This plugin helps you integrate Cookiebot into your project and manage cookies and user consent efficiently.

## What is Cookiebot plugin?

The Cookiebot plugin is a powerful tool that allows you to easily integrate Cookiebot into your Vue or Nuxt application. It provides a simple and efficient way to include your Cookiebot app on your website and configure i18n settings.

More information about Cookiebot can be found on the [official website](https://www.cookiebot.com/){:target="_blank"}.
